# Demonstration image attribution

`IDRiD_001_demo.jpg` is a resized copy of `IDRiD_001.jpg` from the training partition of the Indian Diabetic Retinopathy Image Dataset (IDRiD), distributed under CC BY 4.0.

Dataset paper: Porwal P, Pachade S, Kamble R, et al. *Indian Diabetic Retinopathy Image Dataset (IDRiD).* Data. 2018;3(3):25. doi:10.3390/data3030025.

The image is included only to make the research interface reproducible. It is de-identified and must not be treated as a clinical case.
